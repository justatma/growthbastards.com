The Arkwright Engine

A data demiurge.

It finds the human story hidden in your marketing data, using machine learning to connect language, behavior and outcomes through clustering and predictive modeling.

01what you already have

You have traction.

After a hard start, you have users. And you have a record of nearly everything they did, bought and said. It is scattered across systems that don't talk to each other.

03how it sees

Math that sees what the human eye misses.

Arkwright runs specialized machine learning algorithms: data-crunching formulas built to find structure in records too large and too tangled to read by hand. Two examples.

Prediction

Logistic regression

Estimates the chance of an outcome from a set of clues. Will this customer renew? Will this trial convert?

Interaction

Random forest

Combines many decision trees. Each tree asks a series of questions about past examples. Together, they uncover combinations that a single broad trend would hide.

A setup delay, for instance, might matter far more to first-time buyers than to people who already know the product.

It looks backward at what is known, then forward at what is likely.
  • Which customers are likely to leave, while there is still time to do something about it.
  • Which ads ended up creating the most loyal customers.
04from pattern to proof

A pattern is a lead. A test is the answer.

When the evidence points to a promising change, the team tests it.

Cause

Randomized experiment

Assigns customers by chance to different versions of an offer or experience, then compares the results. Chance assignment is what turns a correlation into a cause.

Belief, updated

Bayesian analysis

Updates the team's understanding as each new piece of evidence arrives, instead of waiting for one verdict at the end. Atma's free ebook explains how →

The question that matters

Is this change likely enough to produce a net profit?

A higher click count alone can't settle that. Arkwright helps answer it.
